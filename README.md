# Preclinical Drills (offline PWA)

Interactive metabolism, biochemistry, physiology, cell biology, embryology, anatomy, nutrition and sports drills in **English, French and German**. The app works completely offline once installed. It can sync your progress between devices through a private GitHub Gist.

**Metabolism (11 drill types)**
- 47 metabolic pathways (glycolysis → sphingolipid degradation)
- Pathway reconstruction, "what happens next", organ metabolism, hormone response, metabolic switching, enzyme detective, missing molecule, clinical cases plus a disease matcher, and energy (ATP) calculations
- 30 clinical cases and 33 inherited metabolic disorders in the disease matcher
- 10 Pathway diagrams: an overview map of central metabolism with clickable pathways, and every pathway drawn as a real diagram (straight, branched or cycle), coloured by compartment. Study mode (tap a step for a plain-language explanation) or fill the empty molecule/enzyme boxes from a word bank
- 11 Organ maps: 9 inter-organ diagrams (fed state, overnight fast, starvation, Cori cycle, glucose–alanine cycle, nitrogen/ammonia, lipoproteins, ketone bodies, heme → bilirubin) with gap-fill exercises, plus a tappable body map with 40 "which organs?" questions

**Biochemistry (5 drill types)**
- B1 Structure builder: build nucleotides, lipids, disaccharides and polysaccharides from their parts; sort and recognise the 20 amino acids
- B2 Enzyme kinetics: Michaelis–Menten, Lineweaver–Burk and allosteric graphs, inhibition types, rate calculations, enzyme drugs
- B3 Buffers & pH: Henderson–Hasselbalch, pI, titration curves, net charge of amino acids and peptides, drug ionisation
- B4 Protein structure: structural levels and bonds, folding and processing, collagen, haemoglobin, misfolding diseases
- B5 Molecule ID: 111 structural formulas (20 amino acids, glycolysis and sugars, Krebs cycle, lipids and ketone bodies, urea cycle and nitrogen compounds, nucleobases and nucleotides, coenzymes, neurotransmitters and hormones). Name ↔ structure, pathway role, net charge at pH 7.4, 29 functional groups highlighted on the structure, 56 "what changed?" reaction pairs, and an **Atlas** to browse every molecule with its formula, groups and role. Skeletal or full C–H drawing.

**Physiology (4 drill types)**
- P1 What happens if…: predict ↑/↓/– for 19 scenarios (haemorrhage, standing up, diuretics, altitude, SIADH, DKA…) and order 10 reflex/cascade sequences
- P2 Graph interpretation: O₂–Hb curves, pressure–volume loops, action potentials, renal clearance curves
- P3 Calculations: cardiac output, MAP, Fick, O₂ content, clearance, GFR, FENa, osmolality, alveolar gas equation, A–a gradient, compliance, anion gap, Winter, Nernst…
- P4 Compare & contrast: 13 comparison tables (muscle types, lung disease, diuretics, DI, AKI, acid–base, autonomic…)

**Cell biology (8 drill types)**
- C1 Cell map: tap the organelle that does a job on a drawn cell (13 structures), or name the job of a highlighted organelle
- C2 Protein trafficking: secretory, lysosomal, nuclear, mitochondrial and endocytic routes as blocks; sorting signals and vesicle coats; what-if cards (I-cell disease, KDEL, ΔF508 CFTR, Zellweger)
- C3 Cell cycle & checkpoints: label cyclin–CDK pairs and checkpoints on a cycle diagram, order the phases, what-if cards (p53, Rb, HPV, vincristine, palbociclib)
- C4 Mitosis & meiosis: phase blocks with chromosome drawings (with or without names), recognise a phase, mitosis vs meiosis I/II table, oocyte arrest and nondisjunction
- C5 Membrane transport sorter: 23 examples into diffusion, facilitated, primary/secondary active and vesicular; drug and disease questions
- C6 Signal transduction: Gs–cAMP, Gq–IP₃, RTK–Ras–MAPK, insulin–PI3K–Akt, JAK–STAT, NO–cGMP and steroid chains as blocks; hormone → system; toxins and drugs
- C7 Apoptosis: fill the intrinsic/extrinsic pathway diagram, apoptosis vs necrosis, clinical cases
- C8 Junctions & cytoskeleton: match junctions, filaments and intermediate-filament markers; drug/disease sorter; clinical cases

**Embryology (8 drill types)**
- E1 Timeline builder: weeks 1–2, weeks 3–8 and fetal milestones as blocks, plus timing facts
- E2 Germ layer sorter: 34 derivatives into surface ectoderm, neuroectoderm, neural crest, mesoderm, endoderm (or a 3-layer version); mesoderm parts; clinical
- E3 Pharyngeal apparatus: arch matrix (nerve, muscles, skeleton, artery), pouches and clefts, DiGeorge / Treacher Collins / branchial cysts
- E4 Fetal circulation: colour-coded diagram (O₂ content) to tap and label; adult remnants; changes at birth
- E5 Heart development: septation steps as blocks, heart tube → adult structures, failed step → defect
- E6 Neural tube & brain vesicles: tappable embryo (neuropores, somites, neural crest), vesicle diagram to label, neural tube defects
- E7 Gut rotation & urogenital tract: midgut rotation blocks, fore/mid/hindgut sorter, sex-differentiation diagram to label, disorders
- E8 Teratogen matcher: 16 drugs and infections, critical windows

**Anatomy (6 drill types)**
- A1 Lesion localisation: tap the injured part of the brachial plexus, upper and lower limb nerve vignettes
- A2 Plexus builder: trace fibres from root to branch as blocks, label the brachial plexus, lumbosacral nerves
- A3 Vascular route builder: arterial, venous, portal, cerebral and lymphatic routes as blocks; anastomoses
- A4 Layers in order: scalp, abdominal wall, lumbar puncture, chest drain, gut wall, epidermis, artery; spermatic cord coverings; hernias and intracranial bleeds
- A5 Foramina & cranial nerves: tappable skull base (11 openings), contents of a highlighted foramen, cranial nerve functions and lesions
- A6 Muscle cards: 34 limb muscles by region – build the card (origin, insertion, nerve, action), “nerve cut: which muscles fail?”, which muscle?

**Across subjects (2 drill types)**
- X1 Compare: 12 easily confused pairs (tight junction vs desmosome, Wolffian vs Müllerian, radial vs ulnar nerve, epidural vs subdural…) sorted into A, B or both, with a side-by-side table
- X2 Term trainer: Latin ↔ everyday names and EN ↔ FR ↔ DE translation for the glossary terms
- Every new drill shows a 🩺 Clinical bridge after the answer that links to a disease or another drill, and every mistake goes to Review (now including the Pathway diagram and Organ map drills)

**Nutrition (2)**
- N1 Nutrition Science Manual: the full 17-unit manual (EU standards, EFSA values) to read unit by unit in your language, with “Quiz this unit”
- N2 Nutrition Science Quiz: 100 multiple-choice questions by unit; each answer links back to the manual unit; misses go to Review

**Sport & exercise (3)**
- S1 Sports nutrition: carbohydrate and protein targets, timing, hydration and heat (↑/↓), IOC supplements, RED-S and iron, g/kg calculations
- S2 Exercise physiology: energy systems and excitation–contraction as blocks, acute exercise response (↑/↓), fibre-type table, training adaptations, VO₂max, RER, lactate, heat, altitude, DOMS
- S3 Exercise calculations: Karvonen zones, METs → kcal, relative VO₂, Fick, sweat rate, energy availability, MET-minutes

**Chemistry & pharmacy (16)**
- K1 Atomic structure: protons/neutrons/electrons (atoms, ions, medical isotopes), atomic and mass number, electron configuration, orbitals and valence electrons
- K2 Periodic table: groups and periods, electronegativity, ionisation energy, atomic and ionic radius (trends and exceptions)
- K3 Chemical bonding: ionic vs covalent (ΔEN), polar vs nonpolar, hydrogen bonds, intermolecular forces
- K4 General chemistry: mole concept, molar mass, balancing equations, stoichiometry and yield, concentrations, dilution, mg/dL ↔ mmol/L
- K5 Acid–base chemistry: pH and pOH, strong and weak acids, buffers, Henderson–Hasselbalch (including blood gases), acid–base equilibrium
- K6 Inorganic chemistry: ions and salts, oxidation–reduction, coordination compounds and chelating antidotes, metal ions
- K7 Organic chemistry: hydrocarbons, functional groups, isomerism, reaction mechanisms, stereochemistry (CIP, R/S)
- R1 Drug structures & functional groups · R2 Acid–base properties of drugs (% ionised, ion trapping) · R3 Drug solubility & dissolution (Noyes–Whitney, logP, Lipinski, BCS) · R4 Drug stability & degradation (t½, t90, storage)
- R5 Stereochemistry of drugs · R6 Pharmacokinetics (ADME: F, Vd, CYP, t½, loading and maintenance doses) · R7 Pharmacodynamics (agonists/antagonists, Emax, therapeutic index) · R8 Pharmaceutical calculations & dosage forms
- R9 Chemistry in medicine & pharmacy: pH, buffers, osmosis, diffusion, equilibrium, oxidation, solubility, stereochemistry and enzymes, each linked to its medical and pharmacy application

Most questions are generated with fresh numbers and graphs each time.

**Study tools**
- Glossary side tab (📘 on the right edge, or the **G** key): every concept, enzyme, molecule, pathway and inherited disorder, plus 76 cell biology, embryology and anatomy terms with Latin names, each with a short definition; “On this page” lists the terms on the current screen. Every entry also has a 🧒 **In simple words** box that explains it for beginners, as if to a 10-year-old (switch it on/off with the 🧒 button at the top of the glossary).
- Building-block chain builder for every ordering task (pathway reconstruction, metabolic timelines, physiology and protein sequences).
- Built-in manual (EN/FR/DE): every drill and tool explained step by step. Open it from the home page, the **?** button next to each drill title, Settings, the sidebar, or the **M** key.
- **Learning path**: every drill is split into units from basics to advanced. Unit 1 is open; a unit is mastered (⭐) when 8 of your last 10 answers in it are correct, which unlocks the next unit. Pathway drills (1, 2, 7, and the tests in 10) use four pathway tiers (core energy → fuel switching → lipoproteins/amino acids/heme → nucleotides/steroids/sphingolipids). Settings → Learning path → Free practice opens everything.
- **Progress page** (📈 in the sidebar, the home dashboard or key **P**): answers, accuracy, active days, streak, units mastered, a 30-day activity chart, the spaced-review forecast for the next 14 days, weakest units, and accuracy plus unit status for every drill.
- Mistakes bank and spaced review: every wrong answer is saved exactly as you saw it and comes back after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days (a wrong answer restarts it). Open it from **Review** in the bottom bar or sidebar. The bank syncs with your scores.
- Today's goal, day streak, continue / random / weakest drill, sidebar on computers, bottom bar on phones, keyboard shortcuts, text size and theme settings.

No build step, no server code, no dependencies: it is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

## 1. Put it on GitHub Pages (one time, ~3 minutes)

1. Create a new repository on GitHub (for example `metabolism-drills`). It can be **public**, or **private** if your plan allows Pages on private repos.
2. Upload **all the files in this folder** (not the folder itself) to the repository root. There are no subfolders, so this works from a phone too:
   - on github.com: *Add file → Upload files*, select every file, then *Commit*. Upload them all in **one** commit.
   - or with git:
     ```bash
     git init && git add . && git commit -m "Metabolism Drills"
     git branch -M main
     git remote add origin https://github.com/<you>/metabolism-drills.git
     git push -u origin main
     ```
3. In the repository, go to **Settings → Pages → Build and deployment → Source: Deploy from a branch**. Pick **Branch: `main`** and **folder `/ (root)`**, then click **Save**.
4. After about a minute the app is live at `https://<you>.github.io/metabolism-drills/`.

> The empty `.nojekyll` file tells GitHub Pages to serve the files exactly as they are. Keep it.

## 2. Install it on Android (works offline afterwards)

1. Open the GitHub Pages address in **Chrome** on your phone.
2. Tap **⋮ → Install app** (or *Add to Home screen*). You can also use the **Install** button on the app's *Sync* page.
3. Open it once while online, so the fonts are cached. From then on it runs fully offline, with no network needed.

Desktop Chrome and Edge can install it too (install icon in the address bar).

## 3. Sync progress between devices (optional)

Your scores live on each device (in the browser's local storage). To share them, the app uses a **private GitHub Gist** in your own account.

1. On github.com go to **Settings → Developer settings → Personal access tokens**.
   - **Fine-grained token (recommended):** *Generate new token*, give it a name, then under **Account permissions → Gists** choose **Read and write**. No repository access is needed.
   - **or a classic token:** tick only the **`gist`** scope.
2. Copy the token.
3. In the app, tap the **cloud icon** (top right) to open the *Sync* page. Give this device a name, paste the token and tap **Connect & sync**.
   - The first device creates a private gist named `metabolism-drills-progress.json`.
   - Every other device finds the same gist automatically: just paste a token (the same one or a new one) and connect.
4. With **Sync automatically** on, the app syncs:
   - when it opens
   - about 15 seconds after you answer questions
   - when you leave it
   - when the connection comes back after being offline

**How merging works:** each device keeps its own counters, and the totals you see are the sum over all devices. Two devices used offline at the same time never overwrite each other. *Reset scores* resets every device at their next sync.

**Privacy and security**
- The token is stored only on the device where you typed it. It is never put in the repository or in the gist.
- The gist only contains score counters and device names.
- If a phone is lost, revoke the token on GitHub. The app keeps working offline.

**No GitHub?** The Sync page also has **Download progress file** and **Import / merge a progress file**. Use them to move progress between devices by email, Drive and so on.

## 4. Updating the app

Edit the files and push to GitHub. Before you push, change the version string at the top of `sw.js` and the matching `?v=` value in `index.html` and `sw.js` (use find-and-replace on the old version). Otherwise installed copies keep using their cached files. When the new version reaches a device, the app shows **"A new version is ready – Reload"**.

## 6. Offline use

After the first visit (or install) the service worker stores every file of the app, so all drills, the manual, the glossary, Review and Progress work with no connection. Progress is saved on the device; GitHub sync is optional and catches up when you are back online. The Google fonts are cached the first time they load; until then the app uses your system font.

## 7. Troubleshooting

- **Stuck on "Loading…" / "The app did not start":** some files are missing from the repository root, or are in a subfolder. Check that every `.js` file sits next to `index.html`, wait a minute for GitHub Pages to finish deploying, then tap **Clear cache and reload**.

- **"GitHub rejected the token":** the token has expired or lacks Gist read/write permission. Create a new one and connect again.
- **Sync shows "Offline":** that's normal without a connection. It catches up automatically.
- **Changes don't appear after pushing:** you didn't change the version in `sw.js`. Change it, push, then reopen the app twice.
- **App not installable:** it must be served over `https://`. GitHub Pages does this; opening the file directly from disk does not.
